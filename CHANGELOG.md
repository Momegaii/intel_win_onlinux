- Corrected the Haswell Gen7.5 PPGTT CPU model against the physical-readiness audit. Added separate `idw_hsw_ppgtt_encode_pde()` and `idw_hsw_ppgtt_encode_pte()` encoders: PDEs carry page-table address 39:32 at bits 11:4 and accept `< 2^40`; PTEs carry page address 38:32 at bits 10:4, map cacheability bit 3 to entry bit 11, and accept mapped pages only `< 2^39`.
